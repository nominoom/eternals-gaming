# ⚡ ETERNALS GGs — Esports & Entertainment Organization

A state-of-the-art, high-fidelity recreation of the **EternalsGGs** website ([original reference](https://my-site-3ln98u3u-eternalsggs.wix-vibe-site.com/)), rebuilt from the ground up as a responsive, reactive web application with cyberpunk aesthetics, dark mode, neon accents, and interactive modules.

---

## 🎮 Recreated Pages & Features

1. **Home (`/`)**:
   - High-energy hero banner with glitch/scanline typography, live trophy counters, and dynamic CTAs.
   - Live esports statistics marquee & ticker (Prize Won, Win Rate, Pro Athletes, Content Reach).
   - "Apex Legends Global Series" featured spotlight with dynamic status badges.
   - Pro Apparel & Gear showcase with quick add-to-cart actions.
   - Official partner marquee (Wix, Arcadian, Zenni Optical, Logitech G, Fourthwall, Blacklyte).
   - Latest multimedia drop & match announcement cards.

2. **Team & Talent (`/team`)**:
   - Tabbed roster switching between **Esports Division** and **Creators Network**.
   - Game filtering: Apex Legends, Call of Duty: Warzone, Marvel Rivals.
   - Athlete cards with real-time stats (KDA, Win Rate, Matches, Signature Role), social links, and bio dossiers.
   - Recent match result cards with scores and VOD links.

3. **Tournaments (`/tournaments`)**:
   - Multi-game championship hubs (Marvel Rivals, Rocket League, The Finals).
   - Prize pools, status badges (`OPEN FOR REGISTRATION`, `IN PROGRESS`), rules, and schedules.
   - **Interactive Live Tournament Bracket** visualizing quarter-finals, semi-finals, and grand finals with live match winners.
   - Squad registration modal with team name and roster slots.

4. **Media & Shows (`/media`)**:
   - Categorized library: Podcasts, Tournament VODs, Frag Movies, and Documentary Vlogs.
   - Interactive search bar and category filters.
   - Built-in floating audio player for podcast episodes.

5. **Pro Merchandise Store (`/merch`)**:
   - Official Pro Collection banner and product grid.
   - Multi-angle product image previews and tag filters (All, Apparel, Gear, Accessories).
   - Interactive size selection (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `2XL`).
   - Slide-out Cart Drawer with quantity adjusters, subtotal calculation, and simulated checkout flow.

6. **About Organization (`/about`)**:
   - Story of Eternals GGs founded in 2021, competitive philosophy, and mission.
   - High-resolution team showcase banner and core pillars.
   - Leadership & executive staff roster with roles.
   - Interactive FAQ accordion.

7. **Contact & Partnerships (`/contact`)**:
   - Categorized inquiry form (Brand Sponsorship, Creator Application, Scrim Requests, General).
   - Direct office coordinates, contact email, and press contacts.
   - Social media grid.

8. **Match Results Archive (`/match-results`)**:
   - Comprehensive match history with scorecards, maps, and VOD links.

9. **Sign In & Authentication (`/signin`)**:
   - Cyberpunk authentication portal supporting Google, Discord, and Email logins.

10. **Athlete Profile (`/profile`)**:
    - Dossier profile page displaying player bio, hardware setup, tournament badges, and editable bio.

11. **Admin Control Center (`/admin`)**:
    - Real-time stat updater: modifying prize money, win rates, or matches instantly updates the entire website live across all pages.
    - Match score submitter and tournament creation panel.

## 🎨 Design System & Palette

- **Neon Cyan**: `#00FFDF`
- **Electric Violet / Purple**: `#7F27FF` / `#6c1feb`
- **Pitch Black & Surface Dark**: `#000000`, `#08090C`, `#0e1017`, `#141724`
- **Typography**: `Space Grotesk` (Headers / Accents) + `Nunito Sans` (Body)
- **Effects**: Glassmorphic blur panels, scanline animations, cyber borders, glowing buttons, hover micro-interactions.
